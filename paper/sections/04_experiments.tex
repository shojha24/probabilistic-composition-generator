\section{Experimental Setup}\label{sec:experiments}

\subsection{Audio Preprocessing and ChordFormer Architecture}\label{subsec:preprocessing_arch}

Audio is downmixed to mono at $f_s = 22{,}050\,\text{Hz}$~\cite{akram2025chordformer}. We extract hybrid CQT spectrograms~\cite{brown1991calculation} with hop $H = 512\,\text{samples}$ ($\Delta t = 23.22\,\text{ms}$), window $N_{\text{win}} = 8{,}192\,\text{samples}$, and $b = 36\,\text{bins/octave}$ ($33.3\,\text{cents/bin}$) spanning $F_{\text{raw}} = 288\,\text{bins}$ ($8.0\,\text{octaves}$) from $f_{\min} = 23.12\,\text{Hz}$ ($\text{F}\sharp_0$, $A_4 = 440.0\,\text{Hz}$). Online pitch shifting steps across $[-5, +6]$ semitones on a $3$-bin grid, slicing $F = 252\,\text{bins}$ ($7.0\,\text{octaves}$, $\text{C}_1 = 32.70\,\text{Hz}$ to $\text{B}_7 = 3{,}951.07\,\text{Hz}$). Log-amplitude scaling clips to $[-80.0, 0.0]\,\text{dB}$, and right-zero-padding by $4\,\text{bins}$ yields $d_{\text{in}} = 256\,\text{channels}$ ($\mathbf{x} \in \mathbb{R}^{B \times T \times 256}$).

In {\small\texttt{confor\_head16.py}}, ChordFormer~\cite{akram2025chordformer} stacks $N_L = 4$ Macaron Conformer blocks~\cite{gulati2020conformer} ($d_{\text{model}} = 256$, $p_{\text{drop}} = 0.10$): (1) half-step $\text{FFN}_1$ ($d_{\text{ffn}} = 1{,}024$); (2) depthwise convolution ($K = 31\,\text{frames} \approx 720\,\text{ms}$, {\small\texttt{SAME}} pad $15$, Pointwise $256 \!\to\! 512 \!\to\! \text{GLU}$, Depthwise $256$, 1-group GroupNorm, SiLU, Pointwise $256 \!\to\! 256$, {\small\texttt{convolution\_first=True}}); (3) 16-head $\text{MHSA}$ ($h = 16$, $d_k = 16$); (4) half-step $\text{FFN}_2$; and (5) LayerNorm. A linear head maps $\mathbf{h}_t \in \mathbb{R}^{256}$ to $\hat{\mathbf{z}}_t = [\hat{\mathbf{z}}_{0,t}^{(97)} \mid \hat{\mathbf{z}}_{1,t}^{(13)} \mid \hat{\mathbf{z}}_{2,t}^{(4)} \mid \hat{\mathbf{z}}_{3,t}^{(4)} \mid \hat{\mathbf{z}}_{4,t}^{(3)} \mid \hat{\mathbf{z}}_{5,t}^{(3)}] \in \mathbb{R}^{124}$ (Section~\ref{subsec:harmonic_sampling})~\cite{jiang2019large,akram2025chordformer}, totaling $|\Theta| = 6{,}123{,}644$ parameters.

During inference ({\small\texttt{extractors/xhmm\_ismir.py}}), posteriors $\mathbf{p}_{h,t} = \text{Softmax}(\hat{\mathbf{z}}_{h,t})$ are decoded via linear-chain CRF Viterbi smoothing over Harte templates~\cite{harte2005symbolic,akram2025chordformer}:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:viterbi_decoding}
  \begin{split}
    S^*(t) = \arg\max_{c} \Bigl\{ &\max_{c'} \left( V(t-1, c') - \tau_{\text{crf}} \cdot \mathbb{I}(c' \neq c) \right) \\
    &+ \log P(c \!\mid\! \mathbf{x}_t) \Bigr\},
  \end{split}
\end{equation}
with $\tau_{\text{crf}} = 2.0$ (multi-head) and $\tau_{\text{crf}} = 30.0$ (MIREX). Fusing the 5-fold ensemble into FP16 TensorRT 10.x on Google Cloud GKE (NVIDIA L4, $24\,\text{GB}$) with dual-stream dynamic batching in Triton achieves sub-$40\,\text{ms}$ latency per $10.0\,\text{s}$ chunk ($431\,\text{frames}$): $2.83\,\text{ms}$ compute at $L=1$, and $10.93\,\text{ms}$ compute ($32.56\,\text{ms}$ round-trip) at $L=13$ peak throughput ($395.75\,\text{inf/s}$, $4.28\times$ speedup).

\subsection{Datasets and Cross-Validation Protocol}\label{subsec:datasets_splits}

We evaluate across two corpora: (1) \textbf{Synthetic Pretraining Corpus}, comprising $5{,}000$ procedural random-mix tracks from Section~\ref{sec:generator} ($460.3\,\text{h}$, $21.8\,\text{GB}$ CQT HDF5), split into $4{,}500$ training and $500$ validation tracks (seed \texttt{42}); and (2) \textbf{Real Benchmark Corpus}, the $1{,}217$-song collection of Humphrey and Bello~\cite{humphrey2015four} spanning $731$ McGill Billboard~\cite{burgoyne2011expert}, $287$ USPop2002/MARL, and $199$ Isophonics (Beatles/Queen) tracks~\cite{mauch2009omras}. Under 5-fold cross-validation (seed \texttt{20190326}), mutually exclusive splits \texttt{s0}--\texttt{s4} ($730/731$ train, $243$ val, $243/244$ full-song test tracks/fold) total $11{,}436{,}357$ evaluated frames ($73.7\,\text{h}$). In Fold~0, majority {\small\texttt{maj}} ($4{,}465{,}981\,\text{frames}$) and {\small\texttt{min}} ($1{,}586{,}913$) outnumber {\small\texttt{sus4}} ($146{,}527$, $30.5\!:\!1$), {\small\texttt{dim}} ($33{,}534$, $133.2\!:\!1$), {\small\texttt{sus2}} ($20{,}531$, $217.5\!:\!1$), and {\small\texttt{aug}} ($15{,}272$, $292.4\!:\!1$), while upper extensions drop from {\small\texttt{9}} ($333{,}926$) to {\small\texttt{\#9}} ($27{,}864$, $221.8\!:\!1$), {\small\texttt{b9}} ($8{,}763$, $705.2\!:\!1$), {\small\texttt{\#11}} ($5{,}804$, $1{,}113.1\!:\!1$), and {\small\texttt{b13}} ($5{,}522$, $1{,}159.3\!:\!1$).

\subsection{Training Regimens: Baseline vs. Sim-to-Real Curriculum}\label{subsec:training_regimens}

We compare two regimens under FP16 mixed precision and AdamW ($\beta_1=0.9, \beta_2=0.999$) on $T = 1{,}000$-frame chunks ($23.22\,\text{s}$): (1) \textbf{Control Baseline} ({\small\texttt{pretrained-\allowbreak benchmark}}), trained from scratch on the $1{,}217$ real songs for $24$ epochs/fold ($B=36$ or $48$, uniform LR $\eta$ over $\{10^{-3}\!:\!12, 10^{-4}\!:\!8, 10^{-5}\!:\!4\}$ epochs, $[-5, +6]$ pitch shifting); and (2) \textbf{Sim-to-Real Curriculum} ({\small\texttt{random-\allowbreak mix-\allowbreak finetuned-\allowbreak second-\allowbreak benchmark}}), pretraining in Stage~1 ({\small\texttt{pretrain\_synthetic.py}}) on $5{,}000$ synthetic tracks for $7$ epochs ($10{,}500$ mini-batches, $B=36$, LR $\{10^{-3}\!:\!4, 10^{-4}\!:\!2, 10^{-5}\!:\!1\}$) with unweighted cross-entropy, pitch shifting, and SpecAugment~\cite{park2019specaugment} ($2$ frequency masks $\le 24\,\text{bins}$, $2$ time masks $\le 60\,\text{frames}$ at $-80.0\,\text{dB}$), then finetuning in Stage~2 ({\small\texttt{finetune\_real\_fold.py}}) on each real fold for $24$ epochs ($B=36$, LR $\{10^{-4}\!:\!12, 10^{-5}\!:\!8, 10^{-6}\!:\!4\}$).

Real training minimizes $\mathcal{L}_{\text{total}} = \sum_{h=0}^5 \lambda_h \mathcal{L}_h(\hat{\mathbf{z}}_h, \mathbf{y}_h; \mathbf{w}_h)$ ($\boldsymbol{\lambda} = [1, 1, 1, 1, 1, 1]$) with inverse-frequency class weights~\cite{jiang2019large,akram2025chordformer}:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:class_reweighting}
  w_{h, c} = \min \left( \left( \frac{\max_k N_{h, k}}{\max(N_{h, c}, 1.0)} \right)^{\alpha}, C_{\max} \right),
\end{equation}
using $\alpha = 0.50$, $C_{\max} = 10.0$, and clamps ($w_{2,\text{\small\texttt{b7}}} \approx 3.602$, $w_{3,\text{\small\texttt{9}}} = 6.000$, $w_{\text{\small\texttt{sus4}}} \ge 6.800$, $w_{\text{\small\texttt{5}}} \ge 6.000$, $w_{\text{\small\texttt{min}}} \ge 2.000$). Stage~2 adds: (1) \textbf{Inverted Discriminative LR} ($\mathbf{s}_{\text{lr}} = [0.2, 0.5, 1.0]$), scaling bottom blocks (Layers~0--1) by $0.2\eta$, top blocks (Layers~2--3) by $0.5\eta$, and output heads by $1.0\eta$; and (2) \textbf{Floor Curriculum Replay}, injecting SpecAugmented synthetic chunks at $\rho(\eta) = 0.150$ ($\eta \ge 10^{-4}$), $0.125$ ($\eta = 10^{-5}$), and a strict floor $\rho_{\min} = 0.100$ ($\eta \le 10^{-6}$) against catastrophic forgetting.
