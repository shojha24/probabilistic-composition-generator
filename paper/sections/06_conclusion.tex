\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}

We presented a Sim-to-Real pretraining framework for large-vocabulary ACR that synthesizes $5{,}000$ multitracks ($460.3\,\text{h}$) via empirical transition sampling, rare-chord quota scheduling, physically constrained voicings, and random-mix augmentation. Pretraining a ChordFormer variant on synthetic audio and finetuning with inverted discriminative learning rates ($\mathbf{s}_{\text{lr}} = [0.2, 0.5, 1.0]$) and floor curriculum replay ($\rho_{\min}=0.10$) yields substantial recall gains over the real-only baseline on rare triads ($+6.64\,\text{pp}$), natural 9ths ($+25.16\,\text{pp}$), and alterations ($79\times$ on {\small\texttt{\#9}}, $7.8\times$ on {\small\texttt{b13}}) while narrowing 5-fold WCSR spread (\url{https://github.com/shojha24/probabilistic-composition-generator}, \url{https://github.com/shojha24/ChordFormer-Artificial-Dataset-Benchmarking}). Limitations of these preliminary results include testing a single quota operating point, omitting an equal-compute/schedule control, and extracting generator transitions/priors from full ChoCo Billboard/Isophonics tables rather than per-fold training splits; future work will enforce fold-isolated extraction, add compute-matched and loss-reweighting ablations, tune {\small\texttt{1}}/{\small\texttt{7}}/{\small\texttt{13}} quotas, and integrate DDSP.
