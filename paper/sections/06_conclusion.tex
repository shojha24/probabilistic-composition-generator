\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}

We presented a Sim-to-Real pretraining framework for large-vocabulary audio chord recognition that synthesizes $5{,}000$ multitracks ($460.3\,\text{h}$) via empirical transition sampling, rare-chord quota scheduling, physically constrained voicings, and random-mix augmentation. Pretraining ChordFormer on synthetic audio and finetuning with inverted discriminative learning rates ($\mathbf{s}_{\text{lr}} = [0.2, 0.5, 1.0]$) and floor curriculum replay ($\rho_{\min}=0.10$) achieves best-in-class recall across rare triads ($+6.64\,\text{pp}$), natural 9ths ($+25.16\,\text{pp}$), and alterations ($79\times$ on {\small\texttt{\#9}}, $7.8\times$ on {\small\texttt{b13}}) on the $1{,}217$-song benchmark while reducing 5-fold WCSR variance. Code and models are at \url{https://github.com/shojha24/probabilistic-composition-generator} and \url{https://github.com/shojha24/ChordFormer-Artificial-Dataset-Benchmarking}. These results reflect a single operating point of the generator's quota space, so further investigation is warranted: future work will tune class proportions for lagging degrees (natural $13\text{ths}$, {\small\texttt{1}}) and integrate differentiable synthesis (DDSP).
